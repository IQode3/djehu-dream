# Data slice

Locked 22 October 2026. Three files, written during the build window, under `data/sample/`. Nothing in those files is copied from a book, a bank, or a private dream.

## The three files

1. **Dream note.** A short dream we write. It has a few figures, a place, and actions the note itself states. It is not the builder's dream. It is not a Hall and Van de Castle report, and it is not one of Jung's dreams.

2. **Passage A.** One short passage from Sigmund Freud, *The Interpretation of Dreams*, translated by A. A. Brill (Macmillan, 1913), [Gutenberg 66048](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/66048). The claim the witness may use: the remembered dream disguises a wish. Freud died in 1939. Brill died in 1948. Life plus seventy has ended for both.

3. **Passage B.** One short passage from a public-domain English translation of Aristotle, *On Dreams*. The claim the witness may use: most dreams are leftovers of waking sensation. This is the stored disagreement. It does not treat the dream as a disguised wish.

## What stays a link

C. G. Jung, "The Psychology of Dreams," in *Collected Papers on Analytical Psychology*, translated by Constance E. Long (1916), chapter XII, on Wikisource. Jung died in 1961. In Austria his text generally runs through 2031. The chapter is not stored. A paragraph from it is not stored.

## What stays out

- The personal dream, including a rewritten version of it.
- DreamBank, the Sleep and Dream Database, and any Zenodo copy of either.
- Any series of children, and any one person's journal.
- Jung's books, the 1916 chapters, and von Franz's books. Titles may be named. Sentences from them are not stored.
- *The Way of the Dream* is the reason the tool exists. It is not a source file.

## Sample mode

`SAMPLE_MODE=true` reads the three local files. The trace says `fallback` when a web page was not used. The graph shape does not change.
